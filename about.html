Our story

Crafted from a love of texture and home.

Crochet Hub is the creative studio of Abili Elmira, a maker based in New York with a love for cozy, meaningful objects. The brand began with the idea that handmade work should feel personal, useful, and beautiful in everyday life.

From warm throws to soft accessories and thoughtful custom pieces, each item is made by hand and designed to bring comfort and character into a home.

I work slowly, with attention to color, texture, and finish. Every order is created to feel considered and intentional—made for living with, gifting with love, and keeping for years.

Handmade

Every item is crocheted with care, one stitch at a time.

Colors and textures are chosen to feel warm, personal, and practical.

Custom
